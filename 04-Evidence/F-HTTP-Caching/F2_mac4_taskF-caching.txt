### 1) Full request — server sends the body plus Cache-Control and ETag
$ curl -sI https://app.team1.test/api/cached
HTTP/2 200 
server: nginx/1.31.6
date: Sat, 03 Oct 2026 11:09:25 GMT
content-type: application/json
content-length: 61
x-backend: B
cache-control: public, max-age=60
etag: "25c29d02c99e446f"
x-edge: edge-mac2
x-upstream-addr: 10.7.8.144:3002

### 2) Conditional request — 'I already have version "25c29d02c99e446f", has it changed?'
$ curl -sI -H 'If-None-Match: "25c29d02c99e446f"' https://app.team1.test/api/cached
HTTP/2 304 
server: nginx/1.31.6
date: Sat, 03 Oct 2026 11:09:25 GMT
x-backend: B
cache-control: public, max-age=60
etag: "25c29d02c99e446f"
x-edge: edge-mac2
x-upstream-addr: 10.7.8.144:3002

-> 304 Not Modified: headers only, no body. The client reuses its saved copy.

### 3) The load-balancing endpoint is deliberately NOT cacheable:
HTTP/2 200 
x-backend: A
cache-control: no-store
